Meet Vanna O'Conner

Founder & Executive Director — education strategist, equity advocate, and leadership coach for school and system leaders nationwide.

Her Story

Vanna O'Conner is a first-generation college graduate who majored in Mathematics — a subject she once struggled with herself. That experience never left her. She built her career around ensuring every student has access to the kind of teaching and opportunities that unlock their full potential.

After graduating from Vanderbilt University, she returned to Memphis, TN, determined to ensure students felt confident and capable in mathematics and had access to opportunities beyond high school. As a classroom teacher, she quickly learned that differentiated lessons and after-school tutoring weren't enough. So she built a solution. She co-created a credit recovery program for Algebra I students and coordinating a college visit day for 300 freshmen.

Over the next 15 years, Vanna expanded her reach, coaching teachers, supporting instructional leaders, and designing professional learning across states and districts. She attended the Beyond Diversity workshop in New York, delivered keynote addresses on the historical roots of educational inequity, and designed communities of practice for leaders working with English Learners and students with learning differences.

Today, as Founder and Executive Director of CenterED Education, she partners with school and system leaders across the country to build the leadership capacity that students deserve.

Our Model

We operate in the gap between knowing leadership and doing it.

The modern school leader's job demands instructional leadership, people management, culture building, change management, and strategic planning — all at once. That's a decision-intensive cognitive role. Training transfers knowledge. Supervision enforces expectations. Neither reliably improves judgment under pressure.

A Thinking Partner

A confidential thought partner who provides real-time feedback for your highest-stakes decisions — not after the fact, but in the moment.

Practice in Context

Not just more knowledge — opportunities to apply and grow inside your actual leadership context, in real time.

Peer Community

Spaces to learn from leaders across different perspectives — and people to call on when you need them most.
