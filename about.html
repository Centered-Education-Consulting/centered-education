Meet Vanna O'Conner

Founder & CEO — national facilitator, equity advocate, and leadership partner for school and system leaders across the country.

Her Story

Vanna O'Conner is a first-generation college graduate who earned a dual major in Secondary Education and Mathematics — a subject she once struggled with herself.

In the classroom, she quickly learned that differentiated lessons and after-school tutoring weren't enough. Her students' success depended on what happened inside and beyond the classroom. So she got to work. She created a credit recovery program that gave Algebra I students another opportunity to demonstrate mastery of the content and organized an annual college visit day where freshmen students explored post-secondary opportunities ranging from four-year universities, community colleges, and trade schools — many of them experiencing and exploring these options for the first time.

Supporting leadership teams, Vanna brings that same belief and dual focus: understanding both the factors that support leadership success inside and beyond their school buildings.

For the last fifteen years, Vanna has served as a national facilitator, designing and leading professional learning experiences for school and system leaders across the country. That work sharpened her belief that leaders need more than knowledge and training — they need the ongoing support and collaboration to put it into practice. Today, as Founder and CEO of CenterED Education, she partners with school and system leaders across the country to build the leadership capacity that students deserve.

Our Model

We operate in the gap between knowing leadership and doing it.

Great leadership deserves great infrastructure. The modern school leader's role demands instructional expertise, people management, culture building, change management, and strategic planning — all at once. Leaders don't fail for lack of effort. They struggle when the systems, alignment, and shared infrastructure to execute together aren't in place. That's where CenterED comes in.
